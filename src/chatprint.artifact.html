How do I export my chat?
  • WhatsApp: open the chat → tap the name → Export chat → Without media.
  • Instagram: Settings → Accounts Center → Your information → Download your information → Messages, JSON format. Pick every message_N.json for a chat.
  • Telegram Desktop: chat menu → Export chat history → format JSON.
  • Discord: export with DiscordChatExporter as JSON.
  • iMessage: run imessage-exporter with -f txt and pick the conversation file.
